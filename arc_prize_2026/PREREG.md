# Pre-registration: Qwen3.8-27B on ARC-AGI-2 public evaluation (written 2026-10-09 02:20 UTC)

Written after six early completions of run g0 had been seen (5 of 6 correct, 23K to 36K tokens, all finished early so biased to easy tasks) and before any pooled result existed. Nothing below was tuned on those six.

## Population and protocol
- 120 public evaluation tasks, 167 test outputs, from arcprize/ARC-AGI-2 data/evaluation.
- Score is the task mean over its test outputs; an output counts as solved if any of 2 attempts matches the gold grid.
- Generator: Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8, vLLM 0.31.0, 4 replicas on 4x A100 80GB, temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, top_k 20, reasoning effort xhigh (default), max 110K new tokens, K=4 samples per output, per-sample seed fixed by (key, sample).
- Reference: NVARC-lineage pipeline on the same population, seeds s0 and s1: 26.81 and 30.97 (mean 28.89, sd of a single run about 2.9 from these two, binomial sd about 2.0).
- Artifacts: artifacts/g0.jsonl on the run box, copied to files/arc_llm/results/ when complete. Numbers are quoted from those artifacts only.

## Contrasts (all paired over tasks, signed mean first, then absolute value; report mean, sd, sem, mean/sem and the smallest effect detectable at 80 percent power, 2.8 x sem)
1. Generator. Unbiased pass@2 of Qwen3.8-27B minus the NVARC mean 28.89. Rule: at or above 38 the LLM route becomes the primary generator; between 30 and 38 it is the hybrid route; below 30 TTT stays primary.
2. Token cap. pass@1 and pass@2 with samples above a cap counted as failures, caps 16K, 24K, 32K, 48K, 64K, 96K, 110K. Rule: the smallest cap within 1.5 points of the 110K value is the deployment cap.
3. Selection among K=4. Vote top-2 (distinct grids ranked by count, ties by shorter mean trace) minus the unbiased pass@2 of a random pair. Reported with its minimum detectable effect; a null with an MDE above the gain a selector could plausibly add (about 3 points) is reported as underpowered, not as refuted.
4. Hybrid. LLM vote top-1 plus NVARC top-1 minus LLM vote top-2, for NVARC runs s0 and s1 separately. Union oracle reported beside it.
5. Hidden-state probe. Logistic probe on states at the last reasoning token and last answer token, layers 16 to 56 step 8, label is sample correctness, folds grouped by task (5-fold), features centred by the training-fold mean. Reported next to every AUROC: the trace-length baseline, a label-permutation floor (200 permutations) and the raw anisotropy of the state pool. The probe counts as useful for selection only if its AUROC beats the length baseline by at least 0.03 and its top-1 pick among samples beats the vote by at least the MDE.
6. Image plus text view (the shared-interpretant arm). Same tasks, same K, same sampling, every grid given as text and as a rendered image. Rule: adopt if the paired pass@2 difference is at least +2 points with mean/sem at least 2.

## Not claimed
- Nothing here says anything about the private half of the competition set; the public evaluation set is a different draw and the competition leaderboard moves by about 2 points between identical runs.
- A null on contrast 5 or 6 with MDE above the plausible effect is reported as underpowered.

## Outcomes (written 2026-10-09 08:55 UTC; numbers are in results/, raw runs in results/raw/)
- Deviations. The run used a 64K reasoning cap instead of 110K after the first batch saturated the KV cache (30 concurrent requests per replica, 364 preemptions, 820 against 2,480 tokens per second) and a SIGSTOP drain lost 93 completed responses to a client timeout; the first run left 79 usable direct samples (results/raw/artifacts/g0.jsonl). The complete direct arm is g0b (K=1, 167 outputs). K=4 was never reached.
- Contrast 1 (generator, direct answers). Direct pass@1 at the 64K cap is 15.1 percent (95% task bootstrap 9.2 to 21.7), below the rule's 30 percent line, so for direct answers TTT stays primary. 73 percent of direct traces hit the cap.
- Contrast 2 (cap). Direct, no forcing: 0.8, 1.1, 4.4, 8.1 and 15.1 percent at 16K, 24K, 32K, 49K, 64K. Direct with a forced answer at 49K: 16.2 percent (n=162 of 167 outputs).
- Contrast 3 and 4 and 5 were not run as pre-registered. Selection among K=4 direct samples was not possible. The hidden-state probe (code arm, 166 outputs, 65 positives, 24 position by layer cells) reached AUROC 0.43 to 0.60 against permutation floors of 0.46 to 0.52 (sd 0.05 to 0.07), and a confidence-feature baseline of 0.47 to 0.52; the maximum is what 24 null draws give, so the result is underpowered, not refuted: it could have seen an AUROC of about 0.65 or more.
- Contrast 6 (image plus text) was not run as a solving arm. The zero-training alignment test on 442 held-out ARC grids gave centred image-to-text R@1 0.400 at layer 40 of 64 against chance 0.0023, which says the views share a space partly; it says nothing about solving.
- Exploratory arm added after seeing the direct results, so its numbers are post hoc on these same 120 tasks and need a fresh set: the model writes a Python function, reasoning is cut at a cap, eight programs are sampled from the truncated reasoning and the answer is chosen by passing every demo pair. Top-1 at caps 16K, 32K, 49K, 63K: 15.0, 31.7, 37.8, 41.1 percent for 24.6K, 41.8K, 58.1K, 70.2K tokens per output (166 outputs, K=1; at 63K the 95% interval is 32.9 to 49.7). Paired against the direct arm at 64K: +26.0 points (sem 4.05, mean/sem 6.4, minimum detectable effect 11.3). Against NVARC top-2 on the same tasks: +15.6 (s0, sem 4.9) and +11.4 (s1, sem 4.9), minimum detectable effect 13.8. Four to six forced records were missing at the 32K, 49K and 63K caps, which biases those figures down by under one point.
- Gating on early checkpoints (stop at the first checkpoint whose program passes every demo) did not beat the fixed 63K cap: 38.3 percent at 77K tokens for checkpoints 16K, 32K, 49K, 63K.
- Hybrid (one LLM attempt plus one NVARC top-1): 50.3 percent with NVARC seed s0 and 49.0 with s1; gain over LLM top-2 alone 7.9 points (sem 2.6, mean/sem 3.1) and 6.7 points (sem 2.3, mean/sem 2.9), minimum detectable effect near 7.

## Outcomes, second block (written 2026-10-09 15:05 UTC; artifacts in results/)
All figures below use the g4 code-arm run on the 120 public evaluation tasks (166 outputs, one sample per output) unless a population is named. They are post hoc on those tasks.
- One trace per task versus one per output (`per_task_eval.json`). Every saved program was re-executed on all test inputs of its task (6 s per call), which recovers forced records that `g4_gated_final2.json` was missing, so per-output top-1 at 49K reads 38.6 here and 37.8 there. Per-task minus per-output top-1, paired over 120 tasks: -0.28 (sem 0.65), -0.28 (0.65), -0.97 (1.31), -0.14 (1.32) points at caps 16K, 32K, 49K, 63K; the smallest effect the test could see at 80% power is 1.8, 1.8, 3.7, 3.7 points. A single trace per task costs under one point here and the contrast is underpowered below about 3.7 points.
- Number of forced completions (`n_completions_eval.json`). Top-1 using the first n of the 8 sampled completions, n = 8, 4, 2, 1: 15.0, 13.3, 11.3, 7.5 at 16K; 31.7, 28.8, 26.7, 22.9 at 32K; 38.6, 35.3, 32.8, 31.9 at 49K; 41.1, 39.4, 38.2, 36.1 at 63K. n = 1 minus n = 8: -7.5 (sem 2.3), -8.75 (2.5), -6.7 (2.1), -5.0 (2.0), mean/sem -3.2, -3.5, -3.1, -2.5. Forcing the close inside one request (one completion) would cost 5 to 9 points; the eight-sample, demo-verified step is where the tokens pay.
- Hybrid at lower caps (`hybrid_fixed16K.json`, `hybrid_fixed32K.json`, `hybrid_fixed49K.json`; NVARC runs s0 and s1 on the same tasks). LLM top-1 plus NVARC top-1: 34.0 and 33.6 at 16K, 43.6 and 43.6 at 32K, 48.2 and 46.5 at 49K. Minus NVARC top-2 (26.8, 31.0): +7.2 (sem 2.6) and +2.6 (3.1) at 16K, +16.8 (3.3) and +12.6 (3.9) at 32K, +21.4 (3.6) and +15.6 (4.1) at 49K. Running both pipelines in one 12 h notebook is not possible at the throughput below.
- Kaggle L4x4 throughput (`kaggle_l4x4_bench_2026-10-09.jsonl`, rebuilt from the printed 30 s windows because the session's own JSONL was lost with the session; the prefix-caching series is incomplete). Qwen3.8-27B-FP8, vLLM 0.26.0 (+cu129 wheel), TP4, fp8 KV, MTP with 2 draft tokens (mean acceptance length 2.4 to 3.0), 20 concurrent requests whose prefixes are real g4 reasoning text spread over 2K to 60K tokens. Decode: 362.1 to 372.8 tok/s (mean 368.0, 6 windows) while KV usage is 0.79 to 0.83, 323.8 to 371.1 (mean 346.3, 8 windows) once it is 0.88 to 0.98. TRITON_ATTN 302 to 309 tok/s. Prefix caching (`--mamba-cache-mode align`, attention block 1600 tokens): 358.4 to 372.3 tok/s with KV usage 0.96 to 0.99 at the same load and dips to 152 to 194 tok/s at 0.98 or more. 32 requested streams over 2K to 49K: 25 to 27 ran, 342 to 402 tok/s (mean 386.4). Prefill 1,534 to 1,613 tok/s for a 31K prompt whether the chunk is 4,096 or 16,384. Single stream 31 to 34 tok/s. GPUs draw 70 to 72 W of the 72 W cap in steady decode. With prefix caching a 6,465-token forced prompt whose first 4,800 tokens were decoded by the same server took 1.5 s with 4,800 tokens served from cache, so the re-prefill of a forced request can be avoided; the cost is that the cache fills at N = 20 for contexts up to 60K.
- Feasible cap. With the measured rates (355 to 450 tok/s decode, 1.6K to 2.5K tok/s prefill, 10% tail loss) the per-task design for 240 tasks needs 37.3K to 28.6K s at cap 32K and 51.5K to 39.5K s at cap 49K against about 42.6K s available. Cap 32K fits at every rate in that range and cap 49K fits only at the top rate, so the deployable program arm is about 32K to 40K, expected top-1 31.4 to about 35 on these tasks. These are public-evaluation numbers with the cap and selection rule chosen on the same tasks, so they are optimistic for the hidden set.

## Outcomes, third block (written 2026-10-09 16:00 UTC; artifacts in results/)
- Second independent run (`results/raw/g5full/artifacts/g5_gated.json`, g5: 4x A100, vLLM 0.31.0, same prompts and caps, new seeds, complete forced data) and the pair analysis (`two_runs.json`, script `analyze_two_runs.py`). The runs differ by less than their noise except at 32K, where the gap is 2.1 sem; a single run's standard error is 3 to 4 points, so the 31.7 at 32K in the first block is the high draw and the two-run mean is 27.7.

- End-to-end solver on Kaggle L4x4 (`results/kaggle_solver_run1/solver_log.jsonl`; script `kaggle_solver/solver.py`, 13,427 bytes): 30 randomly drawn public evaluation tasks (seed 1), one trace per task, cap 36,864 tokens, 30 concurrent traces, eight forced programs, prefix caching on, vLLM 0.26.0 TP4 FP8 MTP2. Task-level top-1 and top-2 25.6 (n=30, standard error about 8 points, so it neither confirms nor contradicts the 28 to 32 expected at this cap from the two-run means). ## Outcomes, fourth block (written 2026-10-09 19:55 UTC; artifacts in results/, raw files in results/raw/fb1 and results/kaggle_*)
All feedback and second-attempt figures use the 333 saved g4 and g5 traces (166 and 167 outputs, 120 tasks, 64K cap, xhigh effort). The control for each is the same trace continued without any change to the 32K reasoning cap, with eight forced programs, scored from the saved candidates. Contrasts are paired over tasks, signed mean first.
- Execution feedback injected into the reasoning (`results/fb_analysis.json`, script `fb_experiment.py`, analysis `analyze_fb.py`). At the 16K checkpoint the best of eight forced programs was executed on the demo pairs; 58 of 333 traces already had a program passing every demo and were not tested; the other 275 got the first failing demo as feedback. Variant A (program, result and cell diff appended inside the thinking block, 16K more tokens allowed): the model closed its thinking and wrote its own program after a mean of 1,047 tokens in all 205 finished units, 0 of 205 had a demo-passing program, top-1 2.3 against 23.2 for the control on the same outputs (99 tasks): -21.0 points, sem 3.7, mean/sem -5.7, smallest effect detectable 10.3. Variant S (A with the think-end token forbidden by logit bias) was stopped after 2 units because both ended by end-of-sequence at 593 and 1,680 tokens without reasoning on. Variant B (a fresh chat turn: prompt, the program as the assistant answer, the feedback as the next user message, 16K tokens of new reasoning, eight forced programs): 151 outputs finished before it was stopped (queue order was shuffled, so they are a random subset; 90 tasks), 11.3 percent had a demo-passing program, top-1 16.1 against 22.2: -6.1 points (sem 4.3, mean/sem -1.4, smallest effect detectable 12.1). Underpowered against differences under 8 to 12 points, not refuted, and not adopted. A minus B on 151 outputs: -13.1 (sem 3.5, mean/sem -3.8).
