# Pre-registration: Qwen3.8-27B on ARC-AGI-2 public evaluation (written 2026-10-09 02:20 UTC)

Written after six early completions of run g0 had been seen (5 of 6 correct, 23K to 36K tokens, all finished early so biased to easy tasks) and before any pooled result existed. Nothing below was tuned on those six.

## Population and protocol
- 120 public evaluation tasks, 167 test outputs, from arcprize/ARC-AGI-2 data/evaluation.
- Score is the task mean over its test outputs; an output counts as solved if any of 2 attempts matches the gold grid.
- Generator: Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B-FP8, vLLM 0.31.0, 4 replicas on 4x A100 80GB, temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, top_k 20, reasoning effort xhigh (default), max 110K new tokens, K=4 samples per output, per-sample seed fixed by (key, sample).
- Reference: NVARC-lineage pipeline on the same population, seeds s0 and s1: 26.81 and 30.97 (mean 28.89, sd of a single run about 2.9 from these two, binomial sd about 2.0).
- Artifacts: artifacts/g0.jsonl on the run box, copied to files/arc_llm/results/ when complete. Numbers are quoted from those artifacts only.

## Contrasts (all paired over tasks, signed mean first, then absolute value; report mean, sd, sem, mean/sem and the smallest effect detectable at 80 percent power, 2.8 x sem)
1. Generator. Unbiased pass@2 of Qwen3.8-27B minus the NVARC mean 28.89. Rule: at or above 38 the LLM route becomes the primary generator; between 30 and 38 it is the hybrid route; below 30 TTT stays primary.
2. Token cap. pass@1 and pass@2 with samples above a cap counted as failures, caps 16K, 24K, 32K, 48K, 64K, 96K, 110K. Rule: the smallest cap within 1.5 points of the 110K value is the deployment cap.
3. Selection among K=4. Vote top-2 (distinct grids ranked by count, ties by shorter mean trace) minus the unbiased pass@2 of a random pair. Reported with its minimum detectable effect; a null with an MDE above the gain a selector could plausibly add (about 3 points) is reported as underpowered, not as refuted.
4. Hybrid. LLM vote top-1 plus NVARC top-1 minus LLM vote top-2, for NVARC runs s0 and s1 separately. Union oracle reported beside it.
5. Hidden-state probe. Logistic probe on states at the last reasoning token and last answer token, layers 16 to 56 step 8, label is sample correctness, folds grouped by task (5-fold), features centred by the training-fold mean. Reported next to every AUROC: the trace-length baseline, a label-permutation floor (200 permutations) and the raw anisotropy of the state pool. The probe counts as useful for selection only if its AUROC beats the length baseline by at least 0.03 and its top-1 pick among samples beats the vote by at least the MDE.
6. Image plus text view (the shared-interpretant arm). Same tasks, same K, same sampling, every grid given as text and as a rendered image. Rule: adopt if the paired pass@2 difference is at least +2 points with mean/sem at least 2.

## Not claimed
- Nothing here says anything about the private half of the competition set; the public evaluation set is a different draw and the competition leaderboard moves by about 2 points between identical runs.
- A null on contrast 5 or 6 with MDE above the plausible effect is reported as underpowered.

## Outcomes (written 2026-10-09 08:55 UTC; numbers are in results/, raw runs in results/raw/)
- Deviations. The run used a 64K reasoning cap instead of 110K after the first batch saturated the KV cache (30 concurrent requests per replica, 364 preemptions, 820 against 2,480 tokens per second) and a SIGSTOP drain lost 93 completed responses to a client timeout; the first run left 79 usable direct samples (results/raw/artifacts/g0.jsonl). The complete direct arm is g0b (K=1, 167 outputs). K=4 was never reached.
- Contrast 1 (generator, direct answers). Direct pass@1 at the 64K cap is 15.1 percent (95% task bootstrap 9.2 to 21.7), below the rule's 30 percent line, so for direct answers TTT stays primary. 73 percent of direct traces hit the cap.
- Contrast 2 (cap). Direct, no forcing: 0.8, 1.1, 4.4, 8.1 and 15.1 percent at 16K, 24K, 32K, 49K, 64K. Direct with a forced answer at 49K: 16.2 percent (n=162 of 167 outputs).
- Contrast 3 and 4 and 5 were not run as pre-registered. Selection among K=4 direct samples was not possible. The hidden-state probe (code arm, 166 outputs, 65 positives, 24 position by layer cells) reached AUROC 0.43 to 0.60 against permutation floors of 0.46 to 0.52 (sd 0.05 to 0.07), and a confidence-feature baseline of 0.47 to 0.52; the maximum is what 24 null draws give, so the result is underpowered, not refuted: it could have seen an AUROC of about 0.65 or more.
- Contrast 6 (image plus text) was not run as a solving arm. The zero-training alignment test on 442 held-out ARC grids gave centred image-to-text R@1 0.400 at layer 40 of 64 against chance 0.0023, which says the views share a space partly; it says nothing about solving.
- Exploratory arm added after seeing the direct results, so its numbers are post hoc on these same 120 tasks and need a fresh set: the model writes a Python function, reasoning is cut at a cap, eight programs are sampled from the truncated reasoning and the answer is chosen by passing every demo pair. Top-1 at caps 16K, 32K, 49K, 63K: 15.0, 31.7, 37.8, 41.1 percent for 24.6K, 41.8K, 58.1K, 70.2K tokens per output (166 outputs, K=1; at 63K the 95% interval is 32.9 to 49.7). Paired against the direct arm at 64K: +26.0 points (sem 4.05, mean/sem 6.4, minimum detectable effect 11.3). Against NVARC top-2 on the same tasks: +15.6 (s0, sem 4.9) and +11.4 (s1, sem 4.9), minimum detectable effect 13.8. Four to six forced records were missing at the 32K, 49K and 63K caps, which biases those figures down by under one point.
- Gating on early checkpoints (stop at the first checkpoint whose program passes every demo) did not beat the fixed 63K cap: 38.3 percent at 77K tokens for checkpoints 16K, 32K, 49K, 63K.
- Hybrid (one LLM attempt plus one NVARC top-1): 50.3 percent with NVARC seed s0 and 49.0 with s1; gain over LLM top-2 alone 7.9 points (sem 2.6, mean/sem 3.1) and 6.7 points (sem 2.3, mean/sem 2.9), minimum detectable effect near 7.

## Outcomes, fifth block (written 2026-10-09 21:40 UTC; artifacts `results/per_task_eval_e1.json`, `results/effort_analysis.json`, raw runs in `results/raw/e1`)
The chat template of Qwen3.8-27B accepts `reasoning_effort` xhigh (default, adds a sentence asking for validation of assumptions and alternatives), medium (no sentence) and low (a sentence asking for brief thinking). Every earlier number in this file is xhigh. This block runs medium (run e1: 120 public evaluation tasks, 167 outputs, one trace each, cap 41,000 tokens, 4 x A100, same prompt and sampling) and a partial low run (e2).
- Trace length. Medium: 97 of 167 traces stopped by themselves and 70 reached 41,000; of the 97 stops 81 have an empty answer and end in the middle of a sentence or grid at 16.3K to 40.8K tokens (median 34.4K), so only 16 are natural finishes. Xhigh: 1 of 83 stops had an empty answer, and 4.2 to 4.8 percent of traces stop within 41K. Low (stopped after 72 traces): 34 stopped (21 with an empty answer) and 38 reached 41,000, so low is not shorter than medium.
- Score. Medium traces were scored at caps 12,288, 24,576 and 36,864 with the programs sampled where the trace was cut, or where it stopped without an answer (`force_end.py`), or its own program. Top-1 (per-output design, task means over 120 tasks): 4.4, 7.4 and 8.5 at 28.2K, 38.7K and 47.0K decoded tokens per output. Xhigh at 32,768 (41.8K tokens): 31.7 (g4) and 23.8 (g5).
- Paired contrast, medium at 36,864 minus xhigh at 32,768, over 120 tasks: -23.2 points against g4 (sem 4.0, mean/sem -5.9, smallest effect detectable 11.1) and -15.3 against g5 (sem 3.6, mean/sem -4.3, 10.0); at 24,576: -24.3 (3.9) and -16.4 (3.6); at 12,288: -27.2 (4.0) and -19.3 (3.6). Medium also decodes 5K more tokens per output than xhigh at 32,768, so the budget does not explain the gap.
- Mechanism. Share of sampled programs that pass every demo: medium 3.0 percent (cap 12K, 1,312 programs), 3.5 percent (24.6K, 1,208), 9.6 percent (36.9K, 736) and 7.4 percent where the trace stopped (648); xhigh 22.9 percent at 32K (g4, 1,280). Medium programs also fail more often to run at all (242, 91 and 42 errors, mostly a missing code block, a missing `transform` or a grid written as code). This test did not separate a weaker reasoning trace from a worse closing step; the 16 medium traces that finished with an answer (12 demo-passing programs, 5 of them correct) are too few to say.
- Decision: the notebook keeps xhigh. Medium and low are rejected at the effect sizes this test could see (about 10 to 11 points), and the observed gap is more than twice that.

## Pre-registration, agent arm (written 2026-10-09 22:40 UTC, before the first 120-task agent runs finished)
Seen before writing: a six-task pilot (budget 24,000 generated tokens, think cap 3,000; tasks 0934a4d8, 135a2760, 136b0064, 16b78196, 1ae2feb7, 2ba387bc) in which 2 tasks were solved (one at 5.3K tokens, one at 21.8K), and the first finishers of the first 120-task run, which hit a harness fault (a turn that had closed its thinking was cut at the think cap) and was restarted. Nothing else.
- Question. Does letting Qwen3.8-27B run Python on the demo pairs between short reasoning turns (`arc_agent.py`) give more correct programs per decoded token than one long trace closed at a cap (`run_llm_arc.py` plus `force_code.py`)? Motivation: the long traces spend most tokens simulating grids by hand, a tool call tests the same hypothesis exactly, and the Kaggle forum reports that teams at the top of the board use an LLM with a live Python session.
- Design. 120 public evaluation tasks, 167 test outputs, one rollout per task (prompt shows test input 0; programs run on every test input), reasoning effort xhigh, think cap 3,000 tokens (a thinking turn that reaches it without a tool call is ended and the model must write a test of its current idea), budgets of 16,384 and 32,768 generated tokens per task over all turns, eight forced programs when a rollout ends on its budget, voting as in the program arm. 4 x A100 80GB, vLLM 0.31.0, temperature 1.0, top-p 0.95, top-k 20.
- Primary measure. Task-level top-1 against the program-arm frontier at equal mean decoded tokens per task (reasoning plus forced programs): 13.75 at 24.6K, 27.7 at 41.8K, 37.5 at 58.1K (mean of runs g4 and g5, linear interpolation; `analyze_agent.py`). One agent run has a bootstrap sem of about 4 points and the frontier has its own run-to-run sd of about 3, so a difference under about 8 points is underpowered, not a null.
- Decision rule. The notebook switches to the agent if its top-1 at its mean token use exceeds the frontier at that use by at least 5 points in both budget runs; otherwise it keeps the program arm. If the agent wins, a Kaggle replica of 120 tasks at the real per-task time has to reproduce the score within 4 points before a submission.
- Planned secondary reads, all post hoc if run: share of rollouts that answer within budget, tokens at which they answer, verified-but-wrong rate, union with the program arm and with NVARC (runs s0, s1) as two attempts, a harness-made first summary call, two rollouts per task.

## Pre-registration, agent variant v5 (written 2026-10-09 23:40 UTC, before any v5 run; the v3 pilot runs b16k and b32k were still running)
Bundle v5 = `--auto-summary --auto-check --all-tests` with eight forced programs reduced to four (`--n-forced 4`), think-cap 3,000, same sampling, same 120 public evaluation tasks, one rollout per task. The bundle holds three changes that are justified by cost before any accuracy is seen: the harness makes the first tool call itself (`summarize()`), which removes the first thinking turn that hits the 3,000-token cap in 94 to 95 percent of tasks (b16k and b32k logs) and the re-prefill of its tokens; the session runs `check(transform)` by itself after every call that defines `transform` (all training pairs and all test inputs, with the differing cells) so the model does not write test code and a program that only works on lists is found; and the prompt lists every test input. Four forced programs lose 1.1 points of tasks with a verified program against eight (expected share 14.7 against 15.8 percent over the 57 forced-step tasks of the partial b16k run) and save 4.7K decoded tokens per task.
- Contrast: v5 minus v3 at the 16,384 budget, paired over tasks (task mean of `correct_top1`), signed mean first, with sd, sem, mean/sem and the smallest effect detectable at 80 percent power (2.8 x sem, about 10 to 13 points at n = 120). Second contrast: decoded tokens per task (reasoning plus forced programs). Third: computed prefill tokens per decoded token from the vLLM counters of the replicas the run used, measured while no other run shares them.
- Decision: v5 replaces v3 for the Kaggle runs if the top-1 contrast is at least -3 points and decoded tokens per task fall by 10 percent or more, or if the top-1 contrast is at least +5 points. Otherwise v3 (the `arc_agent.py` default flags) stays. A contrast inside the detectable effect is reported as underpowered, not as no effect, and the bundle is not split into its parts unless the budget allows a second run.
- Kaggle block: `arc-agent-replica-v4` (default flags, eight forced programs, program-arm cost points) is the replica of the pilot configuration; its top-1 on the 120 tasks must lie within 4 points of the pilot run at the matching token cost to count as a reproduction.
